The course

Prediction with an educational purpose

Use educational datasets to study questions about student learning and pathways. Develop a reproducible modeling workflow, compare methods, and explain what the evidence supports.

Planned software options include R and Python. Shared assignments focus on model evaluation and interpretation.

What you will practice

01

Reliable evaluation

Training and test splits, cross-validation, leakage, and baseline comparisons.

02

Predictive modeling

Regularization, support vector machines, trees, ensembles, and neural networks.

03

Educational interpretation

Subgroup performance, uncertainty, prediction timing, and the limits of model-based decisions.

Semester project

A reproducible study of an educational question

Develop a predictive analysis around a question you choose. Explain the data, compare models, and report their limitations in a research paper and reproducible code.
