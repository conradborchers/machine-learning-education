Planned sequence

Course topics

A shared workflow across methods and programming languages.

Topic order is provisional. The dated schedule will accompany the final syllabus.

01

Prediction and the modeling workflow

Educational prediction tasks, baselines, prediction timing, data splitting, and reproducibility.

02

Regularization and validation

Linear and logistic models, ridge and lasso, elastic net, preprocessing, and cross-validation.

03

Model comparison and feature engineering

Tuning, support vector machines, categorical predictors, and a common evaluation design.

04

Trees and ensembles

Decision trees, random forests, gradient boosting, and educational case studies.

05

Neural networks

Architecture, optimization, regularization, and applications to images and educational outcomes.

06

Uncertainty and educational decisions

Calibration, subgroup evaluation, conformal prediction, reproducible reporting, and project presentations.
